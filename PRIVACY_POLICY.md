# Privacy Policy for BDJobs Auto-Fill

**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

BDJobs Auto-Fill ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the BDJobs Auto-Fill Chrome Extension.

## 1. Single Purpose
The sole purpose of BDJobs Auto-Fill is to help users automatically fill repetitive job application forms across web portals (such as BDJobs, government job sites, and corporate career pages) using templates and profiles created and controlled entirely by the user.

## 2. Information Collection and Storage
- **100% Local Storage:** All profile information (such as applicant name, educational background, contact details, and form field mappings) is stored exclusively on your local device using Chrome's local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **No External Servers:** BDJobs Auto-Fill does NOT operate any external servers, databases, or analytics services.
- **No Transmission of Personal Data:** We do NOT collect, transmit, track, sell, or share any of your personal data, form values, or browsing activity with the developer or any third parties.

## 3. Permissions Used
- **`storage`**: Used solely to save and retrieve your custom form profiles and preferences locally on your browser.
- **`activeTab`**: Used strictly to communicate with the currently active job application tab when you click "Capture" or "Fill form".
- **Host Permissions (`<all_urls>`)**: Required so that the extension can identify standard HTML forms and provide the autofill feature on any job application portal or career website chosen by the user.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The extension does not use any third-party tracking, advertising, analytics, or remote code libraries.

## 5. User Control
You have full control over your data:
- You can create, edit, or delete profiles at any time inside the extension popup.
- You can export your data as a JSON file or delete all data simply by removing the extension.
